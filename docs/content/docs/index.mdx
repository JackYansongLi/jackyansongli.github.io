## About Me

I am an Associate Researcher (副研究员) in Professor [Shiyi Chen](https://faculty.eitech.edu.cn/mech/csy1/main.htm)'s Research Group (陈十一课题组), at the [Eastern Institute of Technology](https://www.eitech.edu.cn/) (东方理工大学).

Before this, I co-founded [LIII NETWORK](https://liiistem.cn/) (三鲤网络), an open source company. I obtained my Ph.D. in the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department at the University of Illinois Chicago. I am very fortunate to be advised by Prof. [Shuo Han](https://hanshuo.people.uic.edu/site/). I received my bachelors in Department of Mathematics at the [Southern University of Science and Technology](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/), where I was advised by (and received many kindly helps from) Prof. [Andy Luchuan Liu](https://staff.uic.edu.cn/andylcliu/en).

Below, I have attached my CV. I welcome any opportunities that align with my expertise and interests. Please feel free to contact me if you are interested in our product or my research: [**[Curriculum Vitae]**](/résumé_yansong.pdf) | [**[Contact: ysli@eitech.edu.cn]**](mailto:ysli@eitech.edu.cn)

## Contributions to Open Source Projects

I co-founded [LIII NETWORK](https://liiistem.cn/) (三鲤网络), an open source company that developed:

<ul>
  <li>
    <a href="https://github.com/XmacsLabs/mogan">Mogan STEM</a>: I am one of the maintainers of Mogan: a WYSIWYG TeX-style editor that can help users type equations 10 times faster than LaTeX. See “<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H46ON2FB30U">A quick tour of TeXmacs</a>” for a 3 mins short introduction.
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="https://github.com/LiiiLabs/goldfish">Goldfish Scheme</a>: I am one of the maintainers of <code>Goldfish Scheme</code>, a Scheme interpreter based on <a href="https://ccrma.stanford.edu/software/snd/snd/s7.html"><code>S7 Scheme</code></a> and <code>C++</code>. This interpreter matches the speed and simplicity of <code>S7</code>, while offering improved support for <code>Microsoft Windows</code>. In <code>Goldfish Scheme</code>, we have implemented the complete set of features from <a href="https://r7rs.org/"><em>R7RS</em></a> and included many functionalities inspired by the <code>Python</code> standard library. <code>Goldfish Scheme</code> will be the foundation for GNU TeXmacs/Mogan in the future.
  </li>
</ul>
